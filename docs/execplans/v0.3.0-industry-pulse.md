## Product decision

The first post-`v0.2.0` slice will convert the implemented BLS Producer Price Index readiness pipeline into a first-class **Industry Pulse** experience.

The product currently explains annual industry cost structure well, but its implemented monthly signal infrastructure remains operator-facing. Industry Pulse will add recent official context without changing the annual output-to-cost ratio, scenario formulas, composite score, or indicator bands.

## User outcome

For a selected or compared industry, the user can determine:

- whether a verified BLS PPI industry series is available;
- the latest observation and release period;
- month-over-month and year-over-year movement when valid;
- freshness and missing-period status;
- the verified NAICS/series mapping basis;
- source, units, seasonal adjustment, provenance, and limitations;
- the monthly history through both a chart and accessible table.

Unmapped industries must receive a clear availability explanation rather than an inferred or approximate signal.

## Architectural boundaries

1. **Signal registry**
   - Typed, validated, officially reviewed BLS PPI mappings.
   - No mapping inferred from a label alone.
   - Duplicate and ambiguous mappings rejected.

2. **Signal service**
   - Typed observations, series summaries, change calculations, freshness, availability, and provenance.
   - Deterministic ordering and explicit missing periods.
   - No provider network call from UI or API request handling.

3. **Offline snapshot**
   - Small, source-attributed, deterministic signal snapshot for the default no-credential experience and tests.
   - Large raw/backfill data remains outside Git.

4. **Additive v1 API**
   - Canonical-only context routes under `/v1`.
   - Typed filters, errors, provenance, release metadata, and OpenAPI coverage.
   - No unversioned alias and no `/v2`.

5. **Streamlit Industry Pulse**
   - First-class tab or equivalent surface synchronized with current industry selection where practical.
   - Latest value, monthly/annual movement, freshness, mapping disclosure, chart, table, and explicit unavailable states.
   - Context label remains visually and semantically separate from annual metrics and scenarios.

6. **Exports and lineage**
   - Structured signal artifact or companion export.
   - Signal lineage remains separate from annual dataset lineage.

## Interpretation guardrails

- PPI is an official producer-price signal, not the dashboard's output-to-cost ratio.
- Price movement does not prove profitability, resilience, distress, insolvency risk, or causation.
- Levels from unlike BLS series must not be compared directly when base periods differ.
- A mapped series may cover only part of a broader industry.
- Missing, stale, revised, and unavailable observations remain explicit.
- Industry Pulse does not feed the annual metric, scenarios, or composite bands in this slice.

## Excluded from this PR

- CES, Census monthly surveys, Federal Reserve G.17, GDELT, or another provider.
- Predictive forecasting, alerts, recommendations, or causal narratives.
- Full NAICS coverage.
- User accounts, saved projects, collaboration, or cloud persistence.
- API v2, removal of compatibility routes, or metric redesign.
- Broad dependency, Docker, workflow, or repository-policy changes.
